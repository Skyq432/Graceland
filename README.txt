DE-GRACELAND FULL-SCREEN MEDIA UPDATE

This update replaces:
- index.html
- admin/index.html

Changes:
1. Images are shown completely without cropping.
2. Portrait and landscape images use object-fit: contain.
3. Tap any content image/video on the approval page to open it across the screen.
4. Tap the mobile preview in /admin to open the full-screen viewer.
5. Videos support portrait and landscape display with native playback controls.
6. A small Back button closes the viewer.
7. Escape also closes the viewer on a computer.
